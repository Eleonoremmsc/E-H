Practical Tips
Booking

Rentals in the South of France are typically available by the week (7-night minimum).

Many gîtes charge extra for linen — worth confirming when booking.

Where to look

The closest villages are Crillon-le-Brave and Bédoin. Caromb, Mormoiron and Mazan are a 10-minute drive away and offer good alternatives.

Many local hosts have their own websites and won't appear on aggregators — searching Google Maps directly often reveals them, with photos and reviews.

Also check: Airbnb · Abritel · Booking.com

While You're Here

Activities

A few of our favourite ways to make the most of Provence, whether you arrive early or stay on after the celebrations.

Walks & Bike
Mont Ventoux
The mythic "Giant of Provence" — drive or cycle to the summit for panoramic views across the Vaucluse.
Col des Abeilles
A quieter mountain pass through beekeeping country, popular with cyclists for its views over the Ventoux ridge.
Ventoux Aventure
Treetop courses and zip lines through the pine forest — fun for the whole family.
Dentelles de Montmirail
Jagged limestone ridges with walking trails and sweeping views over the vineyards below.
Visits & Villages
Château du Barroux
A restored medieval fortress overlooking the plain, a short drive from Crillon-le-Brave.
Abbaye de Sénanque
The iconic Cistercian abbey set among lavender fields near Gordes.
Théâtre Antique d'Orange
A remarkably preserved Roman theatre and UNESCO World Heritage site.
Arles
Roman arena and amphitheatre, in the town that inspired Van Gogh.
Gordes
One of France's most beautiful perched villages, all honey-stone and hilltop views.
Roussillon & the Sentier des Ocres
A walking trail through vivid red-and-ochre cliffs.
Fontaine-de-Vaucluse
The spring source of the Sorgue river, with a shaded riverside village.
Vaison-la-Romaine
Well-preserved Roman ruins and a lively Tuesday market.
Wineries
Château Pesquié
A family-run estate in the Ventoux appellation, known for elegant reds.
Châteauneuf-du-Pape
The Rhône Valley's most famous appellation, with cellar tastings throughout the village.
Beaumes-de-Venise
Home of the region's celebrated sweet muscat wine.
Gigondas
A postcard vineyard village at the foot of the Dentelles.
L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue
Antique markets and canoeing on the Sorgue — famous for its Sunday market.
