    Flying In From Abroad

    Option 1

    1. Land in Paris at Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG).
    2. Take a direct TGV train from the airport to Avignon TGV.
    3. Book a car at Avignon TGV (Sixt, Avis, Europcar — plenty of choice). It's the best way to explore the region.
    4. Crillon-le-Brave is a 40-minute drive from Avignon TGV.

    Important: choose AVIGNON TGV — not Avignon Centre. Avignon Centre is a hassle to reach and difficult to navigate by car.

    Option 2

    1. Land in Marseille at Marseille Provence Airport (MRS).
    2. Book a car at the airport. Crillon-le-Brave is a 1h20 drive away.

    Arriving from Paris

    We don't recommend driving all the way — it takes 6–8 hours, and with the long weekend traffic, it can take even longer. Instead, take the train from Gare de Lyon to Avignon TGV (2h40), then book a car for your time in the area.

    There are plenty of accommodation options nearby, but we recommend booking early — availability fills up fast at this time of year. The closest villages are Crillon-le-Brave and Bédoin, with Caromb, Mormoiron and Mazan a short drive further for more options. Most rentals here are booked by the week in summer, and many gîtes charge extra for linen — worth checking before you book.

    Activities

    A few of our favourite ways to make the most of Provence, whether you arrive early or stay on after the celebrations.

    Walks & Bike
    Mont Ventoux
    ~30 min drive Popular

    The mythic "Giant of Provence" — drive or cycle to the summit for panoramic views across the Vaucluse.

    Col des Abeilles
    ~35 min drive Quiet Adventurous

    A quieter mountain pass through beekeeping country, popular with cyclists for its views over the Ventoux ridge.

    Ventoux Aventure (Zip-lining)
    ~15 min drive Popular Adventurous

    Treetop courses and zip lines through the pine forest — fun for the whole family.

    Dentelles de Montmirail
    ~30 min drive Quiet

    Jagged limestone ridges with walking trails and sweeping views over the vineyards below.

    Visits & Villages

    ~5 min drive Quiet

    A restored medieval fortress overlooking the plain, a short drive from Crillon-le-Brave.

    Abbaye de Sénanque
    ~45 min drive Popular

    The iconic Cistercian abbey set among lavender fields near Gordes.

    Théâtre Antique d'Orange
    ~35 min drive Popular

    A remarkably preserved Roman theatre and UNESCO World Heritage site.

    Arles
    ~1h15 drive Popular

    Roman arena and amphitheatre, in the town that inspired Van Gogh.

    Gordes
    ~35 min drive Popular

    One of France's most beautiful perched villages, all honey-stone and hilltop views.

    Roussillon & the Sentier des Ocres
    ~45 min drive Popular

    A walking trail through vivid red-and-ochre cliffs.

    Fontaine-de-Vaucluse
    ~40 min drive Popular

    Vaison-la-Romaine
    ~30 min drive Quiet

    Beaumes-de-Venise
    ~20 min drive Quiet

    Gigondas
    ~25 min drive Quiet

    A postcard vineyard village at the foot of the Dentelles.

    L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue
    ~35 min drive Popular
